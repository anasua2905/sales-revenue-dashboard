# 📊 Sales & Revenue Performance Dashboard >
End-to-end data analysis project using Python, SQL, Excel & Power BI > Dataset: US Superstore Sales 2014–2017 | 9,994 rows 

--- ## 🎯 Business Questions Answered  

- Which region generates the most revenue and profit? 
- Which products and categories are most profitable?
-Are discounts destroying our profit margins? 
-Who are our top customers and which ones lose money? 
-How has revenue grown year on year?

--- ## 📈 Key Findings 
| KPI | Value | |---|---| 
| Total Revenue | $2,297,201 | | Total Profit | $442,529 | | Total Orders | 5,009 | | Profit Margin | 19.26% | 
- **West region** leads with $725k revenue and 14.94% margin
- **High discounts (20%+)** cause -37% margin — losing $135k!
- **Tables sub-category** loses $17,725 annually - 

**Consumer segment** drives 50% of total revenue - Revenue grew **51% from 2014 to 2017**
